日本語 for Gamers, Uni & Creators

The words you'll hear and use while gaming with friends, at a Japanese university, making new friends, and running a 実況 (let's-play) channel. Verbs and adjectives have their main conjugations built in. Tap "Hide English" to quiz yourself, then tap a blurred line to see it.
